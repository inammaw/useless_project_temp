## 🌟 Overview & Concept

**Tharavadu 95** is a retro Windows 95 simulator reimagined inside a traditional Kerala household (*tharavadu*). 

At its core runs **`AMMA_KERNEL.SYS (PID 0)`**, a living maternal process that tracks your daily actions, screen time, PSC exam preparation, missing Milton bottles, and chore status. Your objective is simple: **survive the day without letting Amma's stress reach 100%**, which triggers the dreaded **BSOD (Martyr Mode)**.

Every sound, window, alert, and dialogue is crafted with cultural authenticity:
- **Dual-Language Dialogue**: Authentic Malayalam Manglish paired with witty English translations.
- **Web Audio Sound Effects**: Synthesized without external audio files (Preethi Mixie grinding, Prestige pressure cooker whistles, rusty iron sit-out gate creaks, Amma's tongue clicks, and retro Win95 chords).
- **Dynamic Dithered Themes**: Real 90s-style pixel dither matrices inspired by Mundukara borders, Kasavu weaves, terracotta tiles, and coir mats.

## 🌧️ The Saree Rescue Mini-Game (`MAZHA_RUN.EXE`)

When sudden monsoon clouds burst over Kerala, Amma's pristine handloom sarees drying on the open terrace are in mortal danger!

### How to Play:
1. Open **Saree Run** from the desktop icon or type `saree` / `mazha_run` in the terminal.
2. Click **Start Rescue Mission**!
3. Rain clouds will release falling laundry items:
   - 🥻 **Golden Kasavu Saree** (+25 pts) — Top priority!
   - 🧺 **Achan's Handloom Mundu** (+15 pts)
   - 👕 **School Uniform** (+15 pts)
   - 🧣 **Thorthu (Kerala Towel)** (+10 pts)
4. Move your basket left and right to catch clean dry clothes before they hit the wet floor.
5. **Score Outcomes**:
   - **Victory**: Catch 10+ garments $\rightarrow$ Amma's stress drops drastically, and fresh Pazhampori is queued.
   - **Defeat**: Dropping clothes into puddles $\rightarrow$ Spikes maternal stress and unleashes furious scolding!

## 👨‍💼 Achan Daemon Intermediary (`ACHAN_DAEMON.SYS`)

When Amma's stress reaches boiling point (>70%), normal commands won't work — she enters a rage cycle where every request is rejected.

### The Paternal Firewall:
- Open **Achan SYS** from the desktop or type `achan` in the terminal.
- Achan is peacefully seated in the sit-out easy chair, engrossed in the *Malayala Manorama* newspaper with reading glasses down on his nose.
- Click **"Acha, Amma is angry at me..."** to initiate mediation.
- Achan lowers his newspaper, clears his throat, and delivers the magic line:  
  *"Shantha, the child was studying on the computer only. Go make one more chaya."*
- **Amma immediately relents!** Stress instantly drops by **35%**, unlocking the system without needing a full reboot.

## 🚨 Survival Tips: How to Avoid the BSOD

1. **Watch the Clock at 4:30 PM**:
   Never delay tea time. When 4:30 hits, brew tea or click Naalu Mani Chaya immediately.
2. **Never Argue with Ammavan**:
   In Kudumbam 95, always reply with respectful greetings. Arguing that NASA doesn't monitor battery levels will provoke an immediate lecture on showing respect to elders.
3. **Keep Achan in Reserve**:
   Save Achan's mediation for when stress crosses 80%. It's your ultimate panic button before the blue screen strikes.
4. **If BSOD Occurs**:
   Don't panic! Click **"Bribe Amma with Hot Elaichi Chaya"** or issue a sincere apology promising to study for the PSC exam to reboot the kernel with a clean slate!
